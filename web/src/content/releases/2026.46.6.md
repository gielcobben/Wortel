---
date: 2026-11-12
title: Scroll and select in Codex after reopening Wortel
summary: Sometimes you couldn't scroll or select in a Codex conversation after reopening Wortel. Wortel now gives the terminal back what Codex asked for while you were away, and ⌘C copies the selection Codex draws.
---

**Scrolling and selecting in Codex.** Codex fills the whole terminal and does its own scrolling and selecting, so when it starts it asks the terminal to pass the mouse on. When Wortel started Codex again in a session you weren't looking at, a long conversation could push that request out of what Wortel keeps for a session while you're away. Opening the session then gave you a terminal that had never been asked, and the mouse did nothing. Wortel now notes those requests on their way out and repeats them when the session comes back. This takes effect once the process that keeps your terminals running has started afresh: close your sessions and quit Wortel for half a minute, or restart your Mac.

**⌘C copies what Codex selected.** With a selection Codex drew itself, ⌘C did nothing: Wortel's Copy command took the key and found nothing selected in the terminal. The key now goes on to the program when the terminal has no selection of its own. Copying text you selected in the terminal works as before.

**Smaller things.** An image smaller than the side column no longer pulls its tab's header down to the middle of the column; the picture is centred at its own size. A file that isn't text no longer offers Diff and Source in its tab, where both showed the same picture.
