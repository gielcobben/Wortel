date: 2026-10-06
title: Tidier menus, and a Clean Up that shows what it cleans
summary: Clean Up Worktrees shows every worktree again, grouped by repository with its state at a glance. The menus are reorganised the way a Mac app's should be, with a new Tab menu and a much shorter Session menu.
---

**Clean Up Worktrees, readable.** In 2026.41.2 the list in Session ▸ Worktree ▸ Clean Up Worktrees… could collapse to nothing, leaving only the button. It now shows every worktree Wortel made, grouped by repository, one line each: tick what should go, see at a glance what's clean and what's kept, with "17 changes" or "In use" on the ones that stay. Rest the pointer on a row for the details, or right-click it to show it in Finder. One switch decides whether the branches Wortel made go too, and only ever the ones with nothing to lose.

**Menus that read like a Mac app's.**

- A new Tab menu holds everything about tabs: new terminal and browser tabs, moving between and moving tabs, reopening, and Close Tab.
- The Session menu is down to what it's about: starting sessions and folders, moving between sessions, the selected session's own commands, and closing it. ⌘1 to ⌘9 still switch sessions, without nine rows for them.
- The worktree commands sit together under Session ▸ Worktree.
- Move To is greyed out when there's nowhere to move the session.
- View is in the order of the window: the sidebar and panels, the source control panes, the browser, then text size.
- Show Sidebar and Hide Sidebar say which one they'll do, Find has its own submenu, and the Wortel menu has Services and Show All, as every Mac app does.

Every keyboard shortcut works as before.
