---
date: 2026-10-05
title: Window tiling is back, and the browser keeps its size
summary: Center, Fill and Move & Resize work again, fn-⌃C and the rest of their shortcuts included. A browser in the side column keeps the width you gave it when you switch sessions or hide the sidebar.
---

**Window tiling is back.** Since the new titlebar, Fill, Center and everything under Window ▸ Move & Resize were greyed out, and their shortcuts (fn-⌃C to center, fn-⌃F to fill, fn-⌃ and an arrow for a half) did nothing. They work again. Dragging a tab in the titlebar still moves the tab, not the window.

**The browser keeps its size.** A browser in the side column could come back at its narrowest after you switched to another session and back. And once you had hidden and shown the sidebar, switching the column between the browser and Files or Changes could make it jump in width. Both are fixed: the column stays the width you gave it. If a session's browser already got stuck narrow, drag it wide once and it stays.
