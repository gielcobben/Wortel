date: 2026-10-09
title: Colours, and a Fork that shows its work
summary: Give a session a colour to come back to it. Fork opens the new session straight away and prepares its worktree while you keep working. A new Permissions popover, and programs in your terminals can now ask for the camera and microphone.
---

**Colour a session.** Right-click a session, or open its "…" menu, and pick a colour: a bar at the edge of its row marks it, say yellow for a review waiting on CI. Only you take it off. From a terminal, `wortel mark yellow` does the same, and `wortel mark none` clears it.

**Fork shows its work.** The new session appears the moment you press Create, and its page says the worktree is being prepared while you keep working in your other sessions. Its terminal opens when it's ready. If git refuses, the page says why, with Try Again. Forking a repository that uses Git LFS works now too: Wortel couldn't find `git-lfs` when it was installed with Homebrew or Nix.

**Permissions.** Wortel ▸ Permissions… shows whether notifications are on and whether Wortel has Full Disk Access, with a button to change each. While notifications are off, a hand with an orange dot in the sidebar says so.

**Camera, microphone and the rest.** A program you run in Wortel that needs the camera, the microphone, your contacts or another app now gets the usual macOS prompt. Before, macOS turned it down without asking.

**Clean Up Worktrees, faster.** The list appears at once with each branch's name, checks several worktrees at the same time, and each row says what it's doing.

Also in this release: a worktree session always shows its branch under its name, never the folder you happen to be in. And dragging a session shows the whole row.
