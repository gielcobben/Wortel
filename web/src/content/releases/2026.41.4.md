date: 2026-10-06
title: A clearer "working", and sections that fold
summary: A session that's working shows three small dots in a soft wave instead of a grey dot, and it's easy to see in dark mode. Pinned and Sessions fold away like folders, and section headers light up under the pointer.
---

**Working, at a glance.** A session with something running used to show a small grey dot breathing slowly, which in dark mode came out nearly black on a dark sidebar. It's now three small dots in a soft wave, the way a chat app shows someone typing: clearly busy, without asking for anything. The coloured dots still mean something needs you.

**Pinned and Sessions fold.** Click the Pinned or Sessions header to fold it away, as you can a folder, and Wortel remembers it. A folded section shows a dot when a session inside needs you, and unfolds by itself when a new session lands in it.

**Headers you can point at.** Folder, Pinned and Sessions headers light up under the pointer like session rows do, so it's clear they're there to click.

Also in this release: every status dot in the sidebar now follows light and dark mode when you switch between them.
