---
date: 2026-11-09
title: No dot for a Codex turn that never happened
summary: Idle Codex sessions could all light up at once with "Finished, your move" while nothing had happened in them. A Codex session now only gets a dot for a turn that really took place.
---

**Idle Codex sessions stay quiet.** After a while, every Codex session in the sidebar could get a dot and a notification at the same moment, though none of them had done anything. Codex writes a short recap under a conversation once it has been in the background for half an hour, and to Wortel that looked like a short turn. Codex also spins its title while it starts up, which looked the same. Claude Code sessions were fixed for this in 2026.44.4. Codex sessions now get the same check: a real turn is always written to the conversation Codex keeps on disk, so when nothing was written, Wortel raises no dot.
