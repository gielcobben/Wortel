---
date: 2026-11-12
title: Screen readers can read the terminal
summary: VoiceOver and read-aloud apps can now read what's in Wortel's terminal, follow your selection and find where each word sits on screen, as they do in Terminal.app.
---

**Screen readers can read the terminal.** Apps that read text aloud or describe the screen, like VoiceOver, ask macOS what an app is showing. Until now Wortel's terminal told them nothing. It now answers as a text area: they get the text on screen (also after you scroll back), the text you've selected with a drag, and where every word sits, so a read-aloud app can highlight each word as it speaks it. They're told when the screen changes, a few times a second at most, and none of this costs anything until an app asks.

**Send Feedback.** Help ▸ Send Feedback… opens an email to hello@wortel.sh with your Wortel version, macOS version and your Mac's chip already filled in, so you can see everything in it before you send.

**Better crash reports, less location.** If you share anonymous usage data, Wortel now notes whether its last run ended cleanly or crashed, and after a crash it sends where in Wortel's own code that happened, as numbers only, with no file paths or names. It also counts which agents you start, from a fixed list, and how often sessions are archived. Usage data no longer includes a location looked up from your internet address.
