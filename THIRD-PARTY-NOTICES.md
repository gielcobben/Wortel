## Ghostty

The terminal itself: parsing, the screen, fonts, rendering, input encoding.
https://github.com/ghostty-org/ghostty

- **libghostty**, built from source at commit
  `5ebe501a63425b13d9952e8c54682c046386e5af` (1.4.1-dev) with
  libghostty-spm's patches and Wortel's own (`macos/GhosttyKit/Patches`). The
  app links `GhosttyKit.xcframework` statically; the keeper (`wortel-keeper`)
  links `ghostty-vt.xcframework`, the same tree without a renderer.
- **Swift ported from Ghostty.app v1.3.1** into
  `macos/Wortel/Ghostty/SurfaceView.swift`, `SurfaceView+Accessibility.swift`,
  `GhosttyInput.swift` and `GhosttyRuntime.swift`. Each file says so at the top.
- **Shell integration, terminfo and themes** from Ghostty's tree, under
  `macos/Resources/ghostty` and `macos/Resources/terminfo`. The shell scripts
  have their own section below, because three of them are GPL.

## Libraries compiled into libghostty

Ghostty's build compiles these into the static library. The list was read off
the macOS build itself on 2026-11-21: the object files and symbols in
`libghostty.a` and `libghostty-vt.a`, checked against Ghostty's
`src/build/SharedDeps.zig` and the `@import`s in its source. Ghostty's other
`pkg/` dependencies (HarfBuzz, fontconfig, glslang, SPIRV-Cross, Dear ImGui,
Sentry, Breakpad, libxml2, gettext, GTK and Wayland libraries) have no symbols
in the macOS library and are not listed.

Where a library offers a choice of licence, the one Wortel takes is named.

library. zf (through Ghostty's theme list) and zigimg (through libvaxis) are

survives into the optimised library could not be told from the symbols, so
they are listed rather than left out.
