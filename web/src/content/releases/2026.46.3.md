date: 2026-11-10
title: No notification for an answer you already read
summary: Reading an agent's answer to the end and then switching to another app could bring a notification with that same answer a few seconds later. A turn you watched finish no longer notifies you.
---

**No notification for an answer you already read.** When an agent finished while you were looking at it, and you scrolled through its answer and then switched to another app, a notification quoting that answer could still arrive a few seconds later. Wortel waits for the screen to settle before it calls a turn finished, and your scrolling kept restarting that wait until you had left. Now Wortel checks whether the session was in front of you after the agent last wrote to its conversation, and if it was, it stays quiet. A turn that finishes after you've left still notifies you, and so does an error or a question.

**Smaller things.** A worktree session with its branch on a second line now has its status dots in line with every other row in the sidebar.
