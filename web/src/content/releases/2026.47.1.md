---
date: 2026-11-17
title: Remind Me brings a session back, and file tabs become windows
summary: Set a reminder on a session and Wortel brings it back to you at the time you choose. File tabs can now be dragged out into a window of their own and back again, and files open in the main area.
---

**Remind Me.** Some sessions you want to come back to later, not now. Choose Remind Me from a session's menu or the Session menu (⌘P finds it too), then In 30 Minutes, In 1 Hour, In 3 Hours, Tomorrow at 09:00, Monday at 09:00 or Custom… for a day and time of your own. While it waits, the row says when on the small grey line under its name. When the time comes, the session gets the unread ring and a banner, or only a flash of the ring if you're already looking at it. A reminder that came due while Wortel was closed goes off when you open it. Closing a session ends its reminder. From a terminal, `wortel remind 20m`, `wortel remind fri 9am` or `wortel remind none` does the same. Usage data now counts that a reminder was set, with nothing about the session or the time.

**File tabs and windows.** A file tab dragged past the edge of the window becomes a preview window where you let it go, as Safari's tabs do. Drag a preview window by its top row onto a tab row and it becomes a tab again, keeping its diff and its view. Preview windows now look like Wortel's main window, with the file's name and Diff · Source · Rendered in the top row.

**Files open in the main area.** A file opened from the Files pane, Changes, History or a link in a terminal went to the narrow side column. It now opens in the main area until you drag one elsewhere, and that session remembers. Hold ⌥ to open it in the other place.

**Fixes.** ⌘J, ⇧⌘T, ⌘F and ⌘G typed into the program when a terminal was focused; they reach the menu again. Grabbing a tab just after opening a file sometimes moved the whole window instead. Folded folders in the sidebar now stack close together. A Markdown file with front matter rendered it as a huge heading; it now shows as a block of YAML.
